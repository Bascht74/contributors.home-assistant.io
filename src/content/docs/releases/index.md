A new version of Home Assistant comes out on the first Wednesday of every month. Before that, there is a week of beta testing. After it, there are weekly patch releases with fixes. That rhythm has been the same for years, and everyone who contributes works with it.

This area explains how that cycle works, and how you can help. You do not need to be a developer for most of it: testing a beta, reporting what broke, and making release notes clear are some of the most useful things anyone can do.

## Who to talk to

- Testing a beta? Join the **#beta** channel on the [Home Assistant Discord server](https://www.home-assistant.io/join-chat).
- Found a bug in a beta? Report it in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/home-assistant/core/issues) of the part that broke. See [Your first contribution](/releases/first-contribution/#4-report-what-breaks).
- The release and beta dates are in the [events calendar](/community/reference/events/).
