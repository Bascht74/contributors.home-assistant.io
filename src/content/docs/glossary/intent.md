"Turn on the kitchen lights" and "kitchen lights on" are different sentences with the same intent: `HassTurnOn`, with the area kitchen and the domain light. The sentences for each language are in the [OHF-Voice/intents](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/intents) repository.
