[Nabu Casa](https://www.nabucasa.com/) is a commercial partner of the [Open Home Foundation](/glossary/open-home-foundation/), and contributes the majority of its profits to it. It makes Home Assistant hardware, and runs [Home Assistant Link](/glossary/home-assistant-link/). See [how the project is organized](/start/organization/) for how the pieces fit together.
