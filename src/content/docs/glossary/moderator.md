Moderators handle reports, remove spam and posts that break the rules, and move topics to the right place. New moderators are nominated by other moderators, and accepted by the community managers of the [Open Home Foundation](/glossary/open-home-foundation/). See [How moderation works](/community/guides/moderation/).
