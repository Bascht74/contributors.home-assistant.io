Feature requests go to the [feature request discussions](https://github.com/orgs/home-assistant/discussions), not to the issues of a repository: issues are for problems. The product team of the [Open Home Foundation](/glossary/open-home-foundation/) follows them, and turns some into [roadmap opportunities](/glossary/opportunity/).
