The resolution center runs checks, records what it finds as issues, and offers suggestions that fix them. It also tracks why a system is unsupported or unhealthy. Users don't see it by this name: the `hassio` integration of Core shows its issues as [repairs](/glossary/repair/). See [The resolution center](/os/supervisor/resolution-center/).
