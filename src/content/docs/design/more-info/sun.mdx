## What it shows

The sun entity tells you where the sun is, and what it does next. Its view is a short list below the state row, which says whether the sun is **Above horizon** or **Below horizon**.

- **Rising** and **Setting**: the next sunrise and sunset, each with how long until it happens on the left, and the time on the right. Whichever comes first is listed first.
- **Elevation**: how high the sun is above the horizon, in degrees.
- **Azimuth**: the compass direction of the sun, in degrees.

The sun above sets in a few hours, so **Setting** comes before **Rising**.

## Design notes

- The times follow the time format of the user, like 24 hour or AM and PM.
- Sorting by what comes next keeps the most useful line at the top, so you never read tomorrow's sunrise before tonight's sunset.
- The sun has no newer more-info design, so the dialog shows the state row at the top, like the [person](/design/more-info/person/) view.
