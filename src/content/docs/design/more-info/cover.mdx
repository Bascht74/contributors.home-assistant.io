## What it shows

Covers come in many kinds, from blinds that stop anywhere to a garage door that only goes up and down. The view picks its main control from what the cover can do.

- **Position**: a cover that can be set to a position gets a tall vertical [control slider](/design/components/controls/). The slider fills from the top down, like the cover itself: a fully closed blind is a full bar. The bedroom blinds in the first demo work like that.
- **Tilt position**: a cover with slats that can be tilted to a position gets a second slider next to it. It shows a marker, not a fill, over a striped background that looks like slats.
- **Buttons**: a cover without a position gets a column of [control buttons](/design/components/controls/) for **Open**, **Stop**, and **Close**. The garage door in the second demo works like that. A cover that also tilts gets the buttons in a cross, with the tilt buttons on the sides and **Stop** in the middle.
- **Open and close only**: a cover that can open and close, but not stop or tilt, gets a big switch, with the icon of an open and a closed cover.

A cover that supports a position and buttons gets both. Two round buttons below the main control switch between the position mode and the button mode. The view starts in position mode.

The state at the top combines the state and the position, like **Open · 70%**. It leaves the position out when the cover is fully open or closed, so you read **Closed**, not **Closed · 0%**.

## Design notes

- The icons follow the device class of the cover, so a garage door shows a garage, and blinds show blinds.
- Buttons that make no sense right now are disabled. The garage door above is closed, so **Close** is grayed out.
- When the integration can't know the real state of a cover (an assumed state), the open and close switch becomes two separate buttons, so you can always send both commands.
- Covers that support a position can show favorite positions below the controls, one tap away. Those come from the entity settings, and are not part of these demos.
- In a cover group, the member covers show up below the controls as tile cards, each with its own position slider.

## In code

The view is `more-info-cover` in `src/dialogs/more-info/controls/more-info-cover.ts`. The main controls are in `src/state-control/cover/`: `ha-state-control-cover-position`, `ha-state-control-cover-tilt-position`, `ha-state-control-cover-buttons`, and `ha-state-control-cover-toggle`. The favorite positions are in `src/dialogs/more-info/components/covers/`. The features are in `src/data/feature/cover_entity_feature.ts`, and the position helpers in `src/data/cover.ts`.
