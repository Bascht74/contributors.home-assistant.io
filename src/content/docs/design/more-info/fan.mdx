## What it shows

The main control of a fan is its speed. What shape it takes depends on how many speeds the fan has:

- **More than 5 speeds**: a tall vertical [control slider](/design/components/controls/) for the percentage, with a power button below it. The bedroom fan above moves in steps of 10%, so it gets the slider.
- **5 speeds or fewer**: a vertical [control select](/design/components/controls/) with one button for off and one for each speed. Fans with up to 3 speeds get names and icons, like **Off**, **Low**, **Medium**, and **High**. Fans with 4 or 5 speeds get numbers.
- **No speed control**: a big on and off switch, with a fan icon for on and a crossed out fan for off.

When the fan is on and has a speed, the state at the top shows it, like **40%**. When it is off, it shows **Off**.

Below the main control, a row of menus holds the secondary settings. Each one only shows when the fan supports it:

- The preset mode, like auto or sleep.
- The direction, **Forward** or **Reverse**.
- Oscillation, on or off.

## Design notes

- The fan slider and the speed buttons take the color of the state, so a running fan is colored and a fan that is off is gray.
- The slider snaps to the steps the fan supports, so it can't land on a speed the fan doesn't have.
- The power button only shows next to the slider, and only when the fan can be turned on and off. With speed buttons, **Off** is one of the steps, so a separate button would say the same thing twice.
- The oscillation menu uses two icons: arrows swinging back and forth for on, crossed out for off.

## In code

The view is `more-info-fan` in `src/dialogs/more-info/controls/more-info-fan.ts`. The speed control is `ha-state-control-fan-speed` in `src/state-control/fan/`, and the switch without speeds is `ha-state-control-toggle` in `src/state-control/`. The speed helpers, like `computeFanSpeedCount` and `FAN_SPEED_COUNT_MAX_FOR_BUTTONS`, and the `FanEntityFeature` enum live in `src/data/fan.ts`.
