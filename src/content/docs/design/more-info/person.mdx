## What it shows

The question you have about a person is "where are they?". The view answers it with a map. At the top is the state row, with the zone the person is in, like **Home** or **Work**. Below it, a map zooms in on where the person is, with a marker showing their initial or picture.

Alex is at home, tracked by a phone. Sam is at work.

For administrators, a **Create zone from current location** button below the map turns the spot where the person is into a new zone. It is not part of these demos.

## Design notes

- The map only shows when the location of the person is known: from their own coordinates, or else from the zone they are in. A person without either gets just the state row.
- **Create zone from current location** only shows when the person has coordinates of their own, since a zone needs a spot to start from.
- The map fits itself around the person, so you don't need to zoom or pan to find them.
- The state is the name of the zone, not the coordinates. People think in places, like **Home** or **Work**.

## In code

The view is `more-info-person` in `src/dialogs/more-info/controls/more-info-person.ts`. The map is `ha-map` from `src/components/map/`. The person domain has no newer more-info design, so the dialog also shows the state row at the top.
