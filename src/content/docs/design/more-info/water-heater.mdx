## What it shows

The main control is the target temperature of the water, on the round dial that the [climate](/design/more-info/climate/) and [humidifier](/design/more-info/humidifier/) views use too: the [control circular slider](/design/components/controls/). A dot on the ring marks the current temperature.

Inside the dial you find the label **Target**, the target temperature in big numbers, and a minus and a plus button. The current temperature is shown as a small label above the dial.

Below the dial, a row of menus holds the secondary settings. Each one only shows when the water heater supports it:

- **Mode**, with the operation modes of the device, like **Eco**, **Electric**, **Heat pump**, and **Performance**. The water heater above is in eco mode, so that is its state.
- The away mode, on or off, with a person icon that walks away when it is on.

## Design notes

- The mode of a water heater is its state. The menu changes the state, and the label on the menu shows it.
- The **Mode** options are sorted in a fixed order, not the order the device reports them in.
- When the device doesn't support a target temperature, the dial turns into a read-only ring with the current temperature and the state, without buttons.
- The dial takes the color of the state, and goes gray when the water heater is off.
