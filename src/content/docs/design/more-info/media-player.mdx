## What it shows

The media player view looks like the now playing screen of a music app, because that is what people expect when they control what is playing. From top to bottom:

- **Cover art**: the picture of what is playing, like an album cover. Without one, a gray square with a music note takes its place.
- **Title and artist**: the title of the media, and a line with the artist or other details below it. Both scroll slowly when they are too long to fit, using [marquee text](/design/components/marquee-text/).
- **Progress**: a [slider](/design/components/slider/) with the position and the duration below it. You can drag it to seek, when the player supports that. It only shows when the media has a duration.
- **Playback controls**: previous and next track, with a big filled play or pause [button](/design/components/button/) in the middle. Repeat and shuffle sit on the outer ends, when the player supports them.
- **Volume**: a mute button and a volume slider. Players that only step the volume get volume down and volume up buttons instead.
- **More actions**: a row of icon buttons for browsing media, joining other speakers, picking the source and the sound mode, and turning the player on or off.

The speaker is playing music. The TV is off: it shows only the empty cover and the buttons to pick a source and turn it on.

## Design notes

- This view has no large state at the top. The cover art, the title, and the play button already tell you what is going on.
- Every control checks the supported features of the player. A control the player doesn't support is left out. Around the play button, a missing control leaves an empty spot, so play and pause always stay in the center.
- The volume only shows while the player is on, since there is nothing to hear when it is off.
- The source and sound mode buttons open a [dropdown](/design/components/dropdown/), with the current choice selected.
- When a speaker is grouped with others, the join button shows a small badge with the number of speakers in the group.
- When the player is unavailable, the view shows only the empty cover with the state, and no controls.

## In code

The view is `more-info-media_player` in `src/dialogs/more-info/controls/more-info-media_player.ts`. Which playback buttons show is decided by `computeMediaControls` in `src/data/media-player.ts`. The features are in `src/data/feature/media-player_entity_feature.ts`.
