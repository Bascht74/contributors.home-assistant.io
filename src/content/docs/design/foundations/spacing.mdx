Consistent spacing makes an interface calm and easy to scan. Home Assistant uses a spacing scale in steps of 4 pixels.

## Spacing scale

Use these tokens for padding, margins, and gaps. The small steps are for space inside components, the larger ones for space between them.

<TokenScale scale="space" />

## Corner radius

Rounded corners are part of the character of Home Assistant. Cards and dialogs are generously rounded, small elements less so. Buttons and chips use the pill shape.
