Home Assistant is built by its community, and much of that work is not code. People answer questions on the [forum](https://community.home-assistant.io/), help someone debug an automation on [Discord](https://www.home-assistant.io/join-chat/), share what they built on Reddit, or organize a meetup in their city. That is contributing too, and it matters as much as any pull request.

This area is for you if you want to help other people get the most out of Home Assistant, keep our spaces welcoming, or bring people together.

New to the community itself? The [community page](https://www.home-assistant.io/community/) on home-assistant.io is the front door for everyone who uses Home Assistant: where to find people, meetups near you, Community Day, and the newsletter. This area is about helping out once you are in.

## Ways to help

- **Answer questions.** Somebody is stuck on something you figured out last month. Your answer saves them hours.
- **Welcome newcomers.** A friendly first reply decides whether someone sticks around.
- **Keep spaces healthy.** Flag what does not belong, and help moderators keep the conversation on track.
- **Share and create.** Show what you built, write a guide, or make videos about Home Assistant.
- **Meet in person.** Join or host a local meetup.

## Who to talk to

- Questions about the community, or a suggestion? Email the Community team of the Open Home Foundation at [community@openhomefoundation.org](mailto:community@openhomefoundation.org).
- Something unsafe or a code of conduct violation? Use the report feature of the platform, or email [safety@home-assistant.io](mailto:safety@home-assistant.io). See [Report a problem](/community/guides/reporting/).
- Content creator? Have a look at the [Creator Network](https://creators.home-assistant.io/).
