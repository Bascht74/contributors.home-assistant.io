Each guide covers one way to help. Pick the one that fits the time and the skills you have.

- [Welcome newcomers](/community/guides/welcome-newcomers/): help someone who just installed Home Assistant feel at home.
- [How moderation works](/community/guides/moderation/): what moderators do, and the steps they take when someone breaks the rules.
- [Report a problem](/community/guides/reporting/): what to do when someone breaks the code of conduct, or when someone does not feel safe.
- [Host a meetup](/community/guides/meetups/): bring smart home fans in your city together.
- [Help on social media](/community/guides/social-media/): help Home Assistant reach more people.
