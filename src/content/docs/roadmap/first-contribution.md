The roadmap starts with problems people actually have. Many opportunities on it began as a feature request. Adding yours, or making an existing one stronger, is the easiest way to help shape what comes next. You need a free [GitHub](https://github.com/) account. New to GitHub? [Use GitHub, without being a developer](/start/github/) helps.

## 1. Search first

Go to the [feature requests](https://github.com/home-assistant/feature-requests/discussions) and search for your idea. Someone may have suggested it already.

## 2. Vote, or add your use case

Found it? Select the upvote arrow at the top of the post. Votes help show what the community values most.

Even better: add a comment with your own situation. What problem would this solve for you, and what do you do today instead? Use cases, research, and evidence help much more than "+1".

## 3. Or write a new one

Didn't find it? Open a new discussion:

1. Pick the category that fits best, like **Dashboards & cards** or **Voice assistants**. Not sure? There is a category for that too.
2. Fill in the form, and explain your idea:
   - What problem does it solve?
   - What is your proposed solution?
   - Why is it important to you?
   - Are there any alternatives or workarounds?

Bugs and support questions don't belong here. The [feature requests repository](https://github.com/home-assistant/feature-requests) lists where to report those.

## What to expect

Not every feature request is implemented. The project focuses on ideas that fit its values: local control, privacy, choice, and sustainability. Some requests are better solved outside Home Assistant itself, like with a custom integration or a dashboard card. Inactive discussions may be closed.

When a request fits, the product team of the foundation can turn it into an opportunity on the roadmap. [From idea to release](/roadmap/how-it-works/) shows what happens after that.

## What is next

- Follow an [opportunity](https://github.com/OpenHomeFoundation/roadmap) you care about, and comment on it.
- Want to help build it? Look at the [epics](https://github.com/home-assistant/epics) and their tasks.
