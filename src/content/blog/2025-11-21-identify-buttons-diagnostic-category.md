---
title: Device identification buttons are now classified as diagnostic
description: Button entities representing a device identification mechanism (using IDENTIFY device class) are now required to have their entity category set to DIAGNOSTIC.
date: 2025-11-21
authors:
  - Artur Pragacz
areas:
  - core
---

Button entities representing a device identification mechanism (using `IDENTIFY` device class) are now required to have their entity category set to `DIAGNOSTIC`.

For more details on entity category, see [the documentation](/core/entities/entity-class/#generic-properties).
